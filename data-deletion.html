Your control over local data: ASAP does not require an online account and does not hold your financial database on a developer server. Most personal financial information is stored on your phone, so deletion is carried out from the app or Android device settings—not remotely by support.

1. Delete local data in the app

  1. Open ASAP: Expense & Money Manager on the device containing your data.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Wipe All Local Data.
  3. If App Lock is enabled, verify using the current PIN as prompted.
  4. Review the confirmation and type DELETE to authorize the wipe.
  5. Allow the app to complete the operation. If the app reports incomplete file cleanup, follow its instructions or contact support.

This operation is intended to remove locally stored financial accounts, balances, transactions, ledger postings, budgets, reminders, SMS review candidates, scan checkpoints and associated private receipt files. The app may recreate essential empty defaults so it can still be used afterwards.

2. Alternative: remove the app's local data

On Android, use Settings → Apps → ASAP → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall ASAP. Android may vary the exact wording by device. This removes the application's private local storage on that device. Confirm any backups you need before clearing data: deleted local records generally cannot be recovered without a saved backup.

3. What is not deleted automatically?

  • Files exported outside ASAP: encrypted .etbak backups and PDF/CSV reports saved to Downloads, Documents or another chosen folder must be deleted separately by you.
  • Copies sent elsewhere: files you saved to Google Drive, email, another cloud provider or another device are controlled by you or the relevant service, and are not erased by the in-app wipe.
  • Google ML Kit diagnostics: limited technical data sent to Google by the bundled text-recognition component are not stored in ASAP's local financial database, and ASAP cannot remotely delete them from Google's systems. See Google's ML Kit disclosure.

4. Request help with data deletion

If you cannot find the deletion setting, need help understanding what is removed, or believe the app operator holds information about you, email aditi.piyush948@gmail.com with the subject ASAP data deletion request.

Explain the issue and the type of data involved. Do not email passwords, App Lock PINs, OTPs, full card details, bank statements or raw SMS. Support can provide guidance and investigate any relevant operator-held information; because ASAP has no developer-hosted financial-data account or database, support cannot remotely access or wipe financial records stored only on your device.

5. Retention

ASAP does not apply automatic 90-day deletion to its local financial ledger. Records stay on-device until you act to delete them or uninstall the app. Backups you created may remain in your chosen destinations until you delete those copies. Any technical diagnostics controlled by Google are subject to that provider's applicable policies.

6. No account deletion required

ASAP does not let users create an ASAP account and does not require sign-in. There is therefore no ASAP account to close; the instructions above apply to local app data.
